Popular engines

Three engines most beginners start with.

Game engine Explanation
Unity A flexible engine for 2D and 3D games, programmed in C#. Great for mobile games and small teams, with a large asset store and plenty of tutorials to learn from. Read more
Unreal Engine Known for realistic graphics and powerful lighting. You can build with C++ or its visual scripting system, Blueprints. Used for big console and PC games. Read more
Godot Free and open source. Light on your computer, easy to pick up, and it comes with its own beginner-friendly language, GDScript. Works well for both 2D and 3D games. Read more

Why use an engine?

Less to build

Physics, lighting and sound are already done, so you can focus on your game.

Many platforms

Make a game once and export it to PC, consoles and phones.

Help is easy to find

Every big engine has tutorials, assets and people who answer questions.
